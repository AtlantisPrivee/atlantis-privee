What to Ask Before an Adjuster Interviews Your Employer

✍️ Atlantis Editorial 📅 November 10, 2026 ⏱ 2 min read

An insurance adjuster wants to interview your employer about missed work. Ask which dates and facts the reviewer needs to verify. A payroll question about hours paid calls for different information from a question about work restrictions or a return-to-work schedule.

Identify which employment records answer the request

Ask whether the insurer needs dates absent, hours scheduled, hours worked, pay rate, payroll amounts or the use of paid leave. Ask whether a signed verification from payroll or human resources would answer the request and whether the insurer has a form. Identify the person who can verify each field.

The federal recordkeeping regulation lists the hours and wage data employers generally maintain for employees covered by that part of the FLSA rules. California's lost-earnings jury instruction is an example of the distinction between establishing earnings lost to date and establishing reasonably certain future lost earnings. Use the records that match the question in your claim. Federal wage and hour recordkeeping rule · California lost-earnings jury instruction

Read any permission form and define the scope

For example, you missed 18 scheduled hours over three days. Your timecard shows eight hours were charged to paid leave, while ten hours were unpaid. Ask payroll to confirm both entries. Ask the adjuster if the request covers those three dates or a longer period needed to establish your usual schedule. If the employer also holds health-related information, separate the wage records from any request for clinical information. HHS explains how HIPAA generally treats employment records and disclosures by covered health providers differently. HHS guidance on employers and health information

You could write, “Please list the dates, the wage fields you need and who will contact payroll. Can the employer answer in writing using our timecards and pay stubs? Please send the proposed authorization so I can review the recipient, scope and duration.”

Save the verification and reconcile it with payroll

Use the Hourly Lost Wages Calculator to organize the hours and pay inputs, then compare them with the employer's reply and the pay period. If the request also includes medical files, identify the scope of the medical authorization separately. If the insurer questions why the absence was included, ask which fact it wants verified.
