Lost Wages After an Injury: How to Calculate and Document Them

✍️ Atlantis Editorial 📅 June 25, 2026 ⏱ 3 min read

A lost-wage worksheet should explain which work you missed, how you know it was scheduled, and how much you were actually paid for that period. Past missed pay and reduced future earning capacity are separate questions. California's civil jury instructions address them separately in CACI 3903C and 3903D. The law and evidence requirements in an individual case may differ. California Civil Jury Instructions

Reconcile the schedule with the pay stub

Start with the affected dates and duties. Use a timecard, shift roster, or appointment book for the expected work. Add the clinician's work restrictions or a dated employer note describing the change in schedule. Compare what you would have worked with what the employer actually paid, including any replacement shifts or paid hours. The U.S. Department of Labor's recordkeeping guidance lists records covered employers generally maintain for nonexempt employees. DOL wage and hour recordkeeping guidance

  • Hourly work. Separate regular hours from documented overtime. The federal overtime rule generally applies after 40 hours in a workweek for covered, nonexempt workers. Use the pay terms and applicable rules for your own job. DOL overtime guidance
  • Salary. Determine which pay periods actually changed. Use the employer's pay basis and any payroll deductions or reductions. A daily rate is useful as a planning estimate when its annual workday assumption is explicit.
  • Variable pay or self-employment. Compare periods of similar length and workload. Separate gross receipts from business expenses and keep the reason for each comparison visible. Cancellations, invoices, deposits, and tax records may help substantiate a claim.

Work through the arithmetic before adding deductions

For example, assume a worker missed 32 documented unpaid regular hours at $24 per hour and four unpaid hours that would have been paid at $36 per hour. The gross scenario is $768 plus $144, or $912. If eight of the regular hours were paid through leave, move those hours to a separate leave column and recalculate unpaid wages. The status of used leave as a claimed loss depends on the applicable rules and facts.

Enter your own rates and hours in the Hourly Lost Wages Calculator. Use the Paid Leave and Unpaid Time Calculator to track different types of time, or the Gig Worker Income Loss Calculator to compare net results for matching periods.

Record reduced work capacity separately

A future claim may depend on more than current hours missed. Keep written restrictions, duration estimates, job duties, and records of changes in assignments or opportunities. Separate a clinician's work restriction from a financial projection of its effect. CACI 3903D offers one jurisdiction-specific example of how loss of earning capacity is framed. California Civil Jury Instructions

If you were injured at work, see how workers' compensation handles work restrictions and wage benefits. For an injury settlement, put the wage worksheet alongside medical, property, and other expense records using the car accident settlement breakdown.
