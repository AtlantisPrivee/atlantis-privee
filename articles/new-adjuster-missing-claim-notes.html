What to Do When a New Adjuster Has Missing Claim Notes

✍️ Atlantis Editorial 📅 November 10, 2026 ⏱ 2 min read

The new adjuster can see your claim number but cannot find an earlier approval or a promise to call back. Send a brief handoff that separates the actions you can prove from the ones you still need the insurer to confirm. The most useful handoff gives the new adjuster an exact place to look and one decision to make.

Reconstruct the events that affect the next decision

Make a three-column record with the date, the action and the evidence for it. For instance, June 4 was the original inspection, June 10 was the shop's supplement upload and June 12 was the former adjuster's call. Attach the inspection report or confirmation if you have it. Describe an oral promise as a dated recollection, and identify any decision you received by email as written.

In Washington, insurers' claim files must contain enough detail to reconstruct relevant events and dates. The rule also gives insureds access to portions of their first-party claim file at reasonable intervals, subject to exceptions for certain records or information. Ask the insurer what records it can provide for your claim and which version of a document is active. Washington claim-file documentation rule

Ask for confirmation at the point where work stopped

In a hypothetical repair claim, the shop's $780 supplement has a delivery receipt and the former adjuster said it was being reviewed. You could write, “The shop sent supplement S2 on June 10, confirmation R173. On June 12 I was told it was under review. Can you confirm that S2 is in the file and tell me its current status and who will handle the next decision?” Add the repair shop's contact only when it helps the reviewer take that step.

If the missing note involved an approval, ask for the approved estimate or amount. If it involved a deadline, confirm the date separately. The NAIC recommends gathering dated records of communications if you need to escalate an insurance complaint. NAIC complaint documentation guide

Save the replacement handoff beside the original records

Use the Insurance Claim Timeline Builder to connect each event to its receipt, letter or call note. When the issue is a missing attachment, give the new adjuster the original upload reference. If the new recap changes a decision, compare it with your contemporaneous notes before updating your timeline.
