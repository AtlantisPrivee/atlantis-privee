When to Speak With a Personal Injury Lawyer

✍️ Atlantis Editorial 📅 June 11, 2026 ⏱ 2 min read

A useful consultation starts with a decision you need to make. You might have a release to review, a denial to challenge, a defendant to identify, or a deadline you cannot confirm. Write down the decision and the document behind it. Courts and state bars describe different ways to find help, and a certified referral service can direct you to a practice area. State Bar of California lawyer referral directory

Seek clarity when a deadline or release is involved

A government claim, a medical negligence claim, and an ordinary personal injury suit may have different notice or filing requirements. If a city vehicle, public property, or public facility was involved, identify the responsible entity early. Ask which date starts each clock and what must be delivered to whom. California Courts deadline guide · California Courts government claim guide

If you received an offer, bring the proposed release and written terms. Ask which claims and people it covers, what is paid and when, and who is responsible for unpaid bills or asserted repayments. See how to compare a settlement with litigation for the issues behind the gross figure. California State Bar guide to fees and costs

Bring focused records when fault, coverage, or losses are disputed

When several companies may be involved, identify their roles and the record connecting each to the event. A truck crash may involve a carrier, driver, owner, or broker whose conduct raises separate issues. A work-vehicle claim can involve an employer's policy and personal coverage with different terms. The truck crash guide and work-car coverage case show how to keep those questions separate. FMCSA company safety records

If the injury affects work or requires future care, bring work restrictions, wage records, treatment recommendations, and any cost estimates. Keep present costs distinct from future assumptions. The Claim Evidence Organizer can link each issue to its source so the lawyer can identify gaps during the meeting. California Civil Jury Instructions, damages series

Prepare for a decision at the end of the meeting

Ask what the lawyer would investigate first, what you can do next, and what would happen only after hiring them. Confirm how fees and costs work under the written terms. The first consultation guide lists documents and questions that make the meeting easier to use. Bring the exact denial, deadline notice, or proposed release if it prompted the call.
