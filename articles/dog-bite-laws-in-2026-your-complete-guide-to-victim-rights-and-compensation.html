Dog Bite Claims: State Liability Rules and Records to Keep

✍️ Atlantis Editorial 📅 June 15, 2026 ⏱ 3 min read

After a dog bite, address the injury and contact a medical professional or public health authority for guidance about infection and rabies risk. CDC advises washing an animal bite wound promptly and seeking appropriate medical attention based on the exposure and injury. Record the animal's identifying information and vaccination information if you can obtain it safely. Follow local reporting instructions. CDC dog bite guidance · CDC rabies prevention guidance

Compare two different legal approaches

California Civil Code § 3342 generally makes a dog owner liable for a bite in a public place or while the person is lawfully in a private place, subject to the statute's terms and exceptions. The California civil jury instruction identifies facts the claimant must establish, including ownership, bite, harm, and causation. The instruction does not require an earlier bite as a general element. California Civil Jury Instructions, CACI 463

New York's Court of Appeals addressed a different framework in Flanders v. Goodfellow. In 2025 it held that a claimant can rely on ordinary negligence for harm caused by a domestic animal. That route exists alongside strict liability when the owner knew or should have known of the animal's dangerous propensities. The decision allows a theory to be considered under its evidence. It supplies no outcome for another incident. New York Court of Appeals opinion in Flanders

Record the incident and the basis for each claim

Write down the location, who was handling the animal, why the injured person was there, and what witnesses observed. Save official animal-control or incident report references. If prior conduct matters, identify the source, date, and specific conduct instead of repeating a general description such as “the dog was aggressive.” If an incident involved a broken gate or a person holding a leash, note who controlled it. The Claim Evidence Organizer can keep facts and supporting records together.

If the injury occurred at a business or rental property, the animal owner's conduct and another party's control of the premises are distinct questions. The premises liability guide explains how to identify control and notice for a condition on the property.

Track treatment and related losses by date

Preserve medical records, itemized bills, payments, and documented time off. If treatment affected a specific task, note the date and how it changed. Make one list of costs already incurred and another of services a clinician has recommended. Check the guide to filing and notice deadlines for why the claim type and state matter.
