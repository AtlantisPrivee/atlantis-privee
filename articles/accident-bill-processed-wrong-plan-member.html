Your Accident Bill Was Processed Under the Wrong Plan Member

✍️ Atlantis Editorial 📅 November 10, 2026 ⏱ 2 min read

A plan notice uses the wrong name for care you received after an accident. First check whether the displayed name is the patient or the policy subscriber. A dependent can receive care under a subscriber's plan. The issue to resolve is whether the claim identifies the person who actually received the service and the correct coverage for that person.

Match the patient details through a secure channel

Compare your itemized bill, coverage card and explanation of benefits, or EOB. Confirm the patient name and service date with the provider. Ask the insurer to verify the member identifier, patient field and relevant plan coverage using its secure portal or a verified contact number. Keep sensitive identifiers in the approved channel. CMS's sample EOB lists patient and subscriber as separate fields. CMS sample explanation of benefits

Ask which record introduced the mismatch. If the provider sent a claim with the wrong member details, ask for the correction date and claim reference. If the provider used the correct details and the payer associated the claim with a different person, ask the payer to explain its correction process. Confirm how each office will address any amount already paid, adjustments and account balances.

Confirm the claim now belongs to the right person

For example, the clinic's account lists the dependent who attended the appointment. The EOB uses the subscriber as the patient. Ask for the corrected plan notice and then check whether the provider posted the new result to the dependent's account. If the insurer requests identity verification, follow its official process.

HHS explains the right to request a change when a covered provider's or plan's medical or billing records contain incorrect information. The office can explain which formal amendment process, if any, applies to the document at issue. HHS guidance on medical and billing record corrections

Ask both organizations which reference to use for the correction and save it beside the disputed notice. If the plan changes the reference, record how it links the old record to the corrected decision.

Record the corrected references

Use the Insurance Claim Contact Log for the office, date and status of each correction. If the correction changes the amount owed, compare the original and corrected claim decisions. If the issuer of the statement is also unclear, verify the billing company and underlying provider.
