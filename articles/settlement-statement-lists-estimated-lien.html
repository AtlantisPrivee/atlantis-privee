What to Ask When a Settlement Statement Lists a Lien Estimate

✍️ Atlantis Editorial 📅 November 10, 2026 ⏱ 2 min read

A settlement statement labels a lien as “estimated.” Ask who produced the figure, what document supports it and whether it will affect the check you are about to receive. An amount in a projection, a sum held in trust and money already sent to a claimant belong on different lines.

Ask what the estimate is based on

Record the creditor or plan, account number, issue date, services included and the amount shown. Ask whether the preparer used an interim notice, a proposed payoff, or a calculation made by the firm. If it comes from Medicare, identify whether you are looking at a conditional payment letter or a demand. CMS describes the interim amount, the process for disputing unrelated items and how the recovery demand is issued. CMS guide to conditional payments and recovery

Ask if the figure is already net of any approved reductions. Obtain a copy of the notice or explanation used so you can compare the next version on the same basis. If more than one entity has a claim, identify the support for each estimate.

Ask whether the estimate changes what you receive now

For example, a preliminary statement lists an estimated $4,900 and the firm confirms it held that amount for a single unresolved item. If the authorized payoff later comes in at $3,750 and there are no other claims to that reserve, the difference to account for is $1,150. Ask the firm to show the amount paid, the date and the disposition of the difference. California Rule 1.15 addresses written accountings and prompt distribution of undisputed funds the client or other person is entitled to receive. California trust accounting rule

A question for the firm could read, “What document supports the $4,900 estimate, how much is actually held, and when will the updated statement show the final payment and any difference?”

Replace the estimate only when the status changes

Use the Settlement Deduction Questions to track the estimate, the amount held and the evidence you still need. If you have competing interim figures, compare them line by line. If the firm has sent a partial check, reconcile it with the actual amount reserved for the dispute.
