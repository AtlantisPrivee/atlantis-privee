A Crash Cost You a Weekly Rideshare Bonus. How Do You Show It?

✍️ Atlantis Editorial 📅 November 7, 2026 ⏱ 3 min read

A weekly payout can drop after a crash in two ways. You can lose trip income during the missed shifts, and those missing trips can leave you short of a promotion threshold. Show the two amounts separately. The trip shortfall is a record. The bonus you might have earned is a conditional part of your estimate.

Save the offer for that specific week

Take a dated copy of the promotion with its start and end times, target trip count, amount, geographic limits and eligibility conditions. Save any enrollment or progress screen, then download the trip and payout records for that period. Uber describes Quest as a promotion that can pay for reaching a trip target within a defined period, with availability and terms that vary. Use your offer and its actual rules for the calculation. Uber's description of Driver app promotions

Show the last eligible trip before the crash and the number still needed. Then record the dates you were unable to work and the documents that explain the interruption, such as a repair intake, vehicle release or documented medical restriction. Preserve the matching platform records when you returned to driving.

Show one week with and without the bonus

Suppose you completed 27 of the required 30 eligible trips before a crash and missed a Saturday shift. In four of five comparable weeks, you made at least three eligible trips on Saturday. Save all five weeks, including the one in which you did not reach the threshold. Explain whether those shifts were in the same area and during comparable hours. Keep the unpaid bonus labeled as a scenario.

If comparable trip receipts averaged $700 with $140 in relevant operating costs and the crash week recorded $470 with $90 in costs, the illustrative net trip difference is $180. A separate $90 bonus would make the difference $270 if the records support including it. Confirm that the baseline receipts excluded incentives before adding one. IRS recordkeeping guidance explains why contemporaneous income and expense records matter for a business. It does not supply a damages formula. IRS recordkeeping guidance

Make the workweek auditable

Use the Gig Worker Income Loss Calculator for the receipts and cost comparison. Attach a separate page for the promotion, its progress and the reason the missed shift would likely have qualified. Describe which costs stopped when you did not drive and which continued. Keep the raw statements so a reviewer can recalculate either scenario.

For coverage questions during different app stages, read which insurance can apply to Uber and Lyft crashes. If the insurer cannot find the promotion screenshots, use a file-level submission inventory.
