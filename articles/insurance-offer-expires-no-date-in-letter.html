The Adjuster Says the Offer Expires but the Letter Has No Date

✍️ Atlantis Editorial 📅 November 10, 2026 ⏱ 2 min read

An adjuster says an offer expires soon, but the letter gives no date. Ask which offer version is active and when a response must reach the insurer. An offer deadline, a policy deadline and a deadline to sue belong on separate lines in your record.

Request the specific cutoff and the document that sets it

Write down when the adjuster mentioned expiration and which letter or figure the call concerned. Ask for the calendar date, local time and time zone. Ask whether the insurer requires a response, signed release or another document by that time, and how it measures receipt. Request the full current offer and any proposed release together.

A follow-up might say, “Your November 4 letter offers $8,000. On November 6 you mentioned a deadline. Please confirm the exact date, time and time zone for this version, the response method and any documents you require by then.” If you need more time to review, ask whether the insurer will agree to an extension and save any written confirmation.

Track legal and policy dates on a separate timeline

Washington's claim settlement rule addresses written notice to certain unrepresented claimants when an applicable statute of limitations or policy or contract time limit may affect their rights during negotiations. It also addresses statements about the effect of a time limit for completing a third-party release or form. Check the rules and policy for your own claim. Washington claim settlement standards

California's code illustrates how filing periods depend on the claim. It has separate provisions for personal injury and for professional negligence by a health care provider. Track any applicable legal filing date separately from the deadline to accept the offer. Confirm both dates promptly when either is uncertain. If the insurer gives you a new date after you ask for more time, save the confirmation and the version of the offer it covers. Ask which time zone applies if the offer closes at a stated hour.

Save the date with the version you received

Use the Insurance Claim Date and Reminder Planner to record the deadline once the insurer confirms it. If the offer exists only in a phone call, capture the amount, scope and proposed release terms. If the adjuster described the amount as a policy limit, request a separate written confirmation of the limit.
