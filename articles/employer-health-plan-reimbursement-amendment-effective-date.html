The Employer Health Plan's Repayment Demand Relies on an Undated Amendment

✍️ Atlantis Editorial 📅 November 7, 2026 ⏱ 3 min read

A recovery letter quotes an employer health plan's right to reimbursement and attaches a page labeled “amended.” The page has no date. Ask for the dated, complete instrument that supplies the language and the plan's explanation of why it applies to the benefits or settlement at issue. A new booklet may describe the plan today while leaving an earlier period unresolved.

Direct the request to the plan administrator

Identify the plan name, plan year and named administrator. A recovery vendor can explain the demand it sent. The administrator is the point of contact for formal plan documents. For plans subject to the relevant ERISA provision, 29 U.S.C. § 1024(b)(4) requires furnishing specified instruments and the latest updated summary plan description on written request by a participant or beneficiary. Ask for the operative version for your case and identify the disputed language. 29 U.S.C. § 1024(b)(4)

Request the complete governing instrument, the amendment that added or changed the reimbursement term, its adoption and effective dates, and any transition provision that the administrator says applies. Ask for the calculation and list of payments the vendor included. If the response quotes a summary, ask how it relates to the plan instrument. Preserve the exact copies and the date each arrived.

Mark the dates separately

Consider a hypothetical crash in March, health benefits paid in May and a settlement in December. The demand cites a provision marked effective July 1. Lay out all four dates, then ask the plan which event triggers the provision and how the effective-date or transition text treats earlier payments. The crash date, treatment date, payment date and date of recovery can answer different questions.

In Curtiss-Wright v. Schoonejongen, the U.S. Supreme Court examined ERISA's requirement for a plan amendment procedure. It upheld the procedure at issue and left compliance with it for further proceedings. The dispute concerned retiree health benefits. For a repayment demand, the practical lesson is to distinguish the existence of a procedure from proof of how and when the cited text was adopted. U.S. Supreme Court opinion in Curtiss-Wright

Match the claim amount to the plan text

Ask for a table tying each payment to a service date and the claim reference, and for the provision the plan uses to calculate reimbursement. A narrow request makes it easier to see whether the remaining question is the version, the expense list or the amount. The Records Request Letter Builder can organize the document names and date ranges. If counsel handles the settlement, send the demand and the dated versions for review before the allocation of funds.
