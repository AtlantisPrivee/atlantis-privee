Uber and Lyft Accidents: Which Insurance Applies?

✍️ Atlantis Editorial 📅 June 15, 2026 ⏱ 3 min read

After a rideshare crash, save the trip receipt, pickup and drop-off information, and any report confirmation. The timestamp that matters for coverage can be the moment the crash happened relative to when the driver went online, accepted the trip, or ended it. A driver's platform membership alone cannot establish the status at the time of a crash. The companies publish coverage summaries organized by these stages. Uber insurance summary · Lyft insurance resources

Start with the app status at the time of impact

  • Offline. Check the driver's policy, crash report, and trip history. Ask which personal or commercial policy covered the use of the car.
  • Online and waiting. Compare the app activity log or dispatch record with the crash time. Ask what liability coverage applied while the driver was available.
  • Ride accepted or underway. Match the trip receipt and platform report to the acceptance and trip timestamps. Ask which policy and limits were in force for that trip.

Coverage is state- and policy-specific. California's regulator distinguishes the time spent waiting online, the trip to pick up a matched passenger, and the time with the passenger in the vehicle. The platform's current state disclosure and the actual policy or certificate help identify the applicable limits, endorsements, and exclusions. Keep liability, uninsured or underinsured motorist coverage, and damage to the driver's vehicle on separate lines. California Public Utilities Commission TNC insurance requirements · Lyft state and local disclosures

Match your role to your claim number

A passenger can usually identify the trip from the receipt. Preserve it and report through the platform's crash process. If you were in another vehicle or walking, your starting evidence may be the driver's identity, police report, and insurer correspondence rather than a trip receipt. A rideshare driver should also preserve app activity and any rideshare endorsement to a personal policy. When two apps may have been open, record the evidence for each app separately and ask each insurer to identify the period it evaluated.

For each claim number, record the insurer, the specific coverage under review, and which documents it requested. The Insurance Claim Contact Log keeps multiple contacts from becoming one running conversation. The work-vehicle underinsured motorist case illustrates why an employer or vehicle policy and a personal policy can produce different answers.

A driver who missed a promotion threshold can document the offered terms, eligible trip count and comparable shifts separately from the usual trip earnings. How to document a missed weekly rideshare bonus.

Keep the injury and repair records separate

A statement about third-party liability coverage may leave your medical payments, deductibles, and repair expenses unresolved. Ask which payment covers which loss, whether it is subject to a limit or deductible, and what the insurer needs to confirm eligibility. When a collision falls between accepting the request and picking up the passenger, compare the platform's status changes with the crash timestamp.
