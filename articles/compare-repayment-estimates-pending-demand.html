How to Compare Two Repayment Estimates While a Demand Is Pending

✍️ Atlantis Editorial 📅 November 10, 2026 ⏱ 2 min read

Two documents show different amounts that might be repaid out of a settlement. Before comparing the totals, identify who issued each figure, what period it covers and which stage of the process it represents. The difference can come from new charges, an accepted dispute, a formula for recovery costs, or two documents covering separate obligations.

Compare the same obligation on both documents

Record the issuing entity, reference number, statement date, last service or payment date covered, and the services listed. Put increases and reductions on different rows. Ask which lines were added, removed or changed and whether the changes are final or pending review.

For Medicare recovery from a beneficiary, CMS says a conditional payment letter reports an interim amount and explains how to dispute unrelated items. The Benefits Coordination & Recovery Center typically issues a recovery demand after receiving and processing settlement information. A separate pre-settlement process is available in eligible cases and has its own steps. CMS Medicare recovery process · CMS demand calculation options

Reconcile the difference before running a net estimate

Suppose the first hypothetical letter shows $4,800 and the updated one shows $5,200. The new letter adds an $850 therapy payment and removes a $450 unrelated item after a dispute. Those changes explain the $400 increase. Track any request still outstanding separately, along with a date to check for the response.

Ask whether either total reflects any applicable deduction for fees and expenses. The Medicare recovery formula in 42 CFR § 411.37 addresses qualifying procurement costs in defined situations. For another payer, use the plan terms and applicable law for that obligation. Compare estimates that use the same assumptions before deciding what the change means. Federal rule on Medicare recovery and procurement costs

Keep the estimates as separate scenarios

Use the net settlement offer comparison tool to compare the same gross recovery with each possible deduction, labeling the results as estimates. Replace the figure when an authorized final demand or payoff arrives. If your settlement statement already shows a reserve, check who approved the estimate and how the difference will be released.
