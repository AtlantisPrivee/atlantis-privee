How to List Missing Documents in an Insurance Claim Follow-Up

✍️ Atlantis Editorial 📅 November 10, 2026 ⏱ 2 min read

An adjuster says documents are missing, but several people have been sending files. Turn the request into a status list with a separate entry for each record. Identify who has it, when it moved and the action that will get it to the reviewer.

Give each document an owner and a status

Use the document name, service or issue date, sender, delivery reference and current status. A document can be ready to send, sent with delivery confirmed, sent with receipt pending, or still being prepared by someone else. Ask the insurer to distinguish a missing attachment from a file it received but could not open or use.

Keep the list in the body of your message. Reference an approved upload location for sensitive attachments and keep the full identifiers in the insurer's secure channel. The NAIC's complaint guide recommends gathering bills, records and a dated record of communications. Those same records make it easier to answer a follow-up request before escalation. NAIC complaint documentation checklist

Write the next action into the message

Suppose a claim needs three items. Your message could say, “Claim 5231. The July 9 repair estimate was uploaded on July 10, receipt Q42. Please confirm it opens. The July 12 supplemental photos are with the repair shop, which expects to upload them Friday. Please confirm whether you need the June 28 invoice or the June 28 payment receipt so I can send the right file.”

If the missing item is holding up only one part of the claim, ask the adjuster to name that part. In Washington, a claims communication that reasonably calls for a response is subject to the applicable reply standard under WAC 284-30-360. The rule distinguishes individual policies from group contracts. Washington claims communication standard

Close each item when you have a receipt or decision

Add the insurer's answer beside the item it addresses. Use the Insurance Claim Follow-Up Email Builder to draft the next request from the remaining entries. If the same attachment appears on another missing list, link each resend to the original receipt. For a revised estimate that is already in the insurer's queue, ask for its review or inspection date.
