Your California Employer Gave You a DWC-1 Without a Date

✍️ Atlantis Editorial 📅 November 7, 2026 ⏱ 2 min read

The employer hands you a workers' compensation form with its date fields blank. Write down when you received it and complete the employee portion with the facts you can verify. The date the form was provided and the date the completed form reached the employer are separate entries on California's DWC-1.

Match each date to what happened

Get the current form from California's Division of Workers' Compensation. The employer section distinguishes when the employer first knew about the injury, when it provided the form and when it received the completed form. Keep a copy of what you received, including any email, envelope or other delivery record. Enter your own injury and signature information in the employee section. Ask the employer to fill its own section. California DWC-1 claim form and instructions

Return the completed employee portion using the process in the DWC's instructions and keep proof of delivery or receipt. The DWC recommends keeping a copy and describes certified mail with a return receipt if you submit by mail. If you hand it in, note the date, person and location and ask for written acknowledgment. California DWC claim filing guide

Ask for the dated copy after return

California's regulation requires the employer to date the form and provide a dated copy to the employee and claims administrator within one working day after receiving the completed claim form. For example, if the form was handed out Monday and delivered back on Wednesday, record both dates and ask for the copy reflecting Wednesday's receipt. Save your receipt confirmation with the form. California Code of Regulations, title 8, § 10140

If the copy has the wrong date or you did not receive one, send a short written request identifying the injury and when you returned the form. Attach your receipt record and ask for the completed copy and claims administrator contact. California DWC's filing guide explains where to get assistance if you need help with the process.

Keep the filing timeline with the injury records

Use the Insurance Claim Date and Reminder Planner to track notice of injury, receipt of the blank form, return and receipt of the dated copy. If the injury involved rented equipment, see what to request from the equipment supplier and site. If you went back on a limited schedule, preserve the restrictions, work hours and pay records using the half-day return guide.
