How to Calculate a Settlement Fee on One Coverage Payment

✍️ Atlantis Editorial 📅 November 10, 2026 ⏱ 2 min read

Your insurer issued one payment under collision and another under injury coverage. If the attorney fee applies to only one of those amounts, list each payment separately and identify the fee base from the signed agreement. The fact that the payments concern one accident is only one part of that review.

Match the scope of the agreement to the payment

Collect the engagement agreement and any amendments, the insurer's payment notices and the settlement statement. Note each coverage, payer and payee. Ask which agreement provision applies to the payment and whether the percentage is calculated before or after allowable costs. Keep a cost charged to the case separate from the percentage calculation.

ABA Model Rule 1.5 describes how a written contingency fee agreement should explain the calculation, expenses and whether expenses are deducted before or after the fee is calculated. Check the rule adopted in your jurisdiction. The California State Bar's consumer guide explains how to review that distinction in a fee agreement. ABA Model Rule 1.5 on fees · California State Bar guide to fee agreements

Work the calculation one payment at a time

Here is a hypothetical example. A $6,000 collision payment is outside the scope of the fee agreement and a $20,000 injury payment is subject to a 30% fee on gross proceeds. The fee is $6,000, calculated on the $20,000 only. If the injury payment also covers $500 in agreed case costs and $2,500 in a separate repayment, that payment contributes $11,000 to the client's net. With the $6,000 collision payment, the total net from the two payments is $17,000.

If the agreement instead calculates the fee after the $500 cost, the fee on $19,500 would be $5,850. Keep the underlying payment amounts fixed so you can see which difference comes from the fee clause. Ask the firm to identify any allocation rule or other term that changes the inputs.

Check what actually reached each payee

Use the Net Settlement Calculator for each payment, then compare the figures with the combined settlement statement. If the coverage on a check is unclear, request the insurer's payment description. If a filing fee was collected from one payment, trace the cost and fee base separately.
