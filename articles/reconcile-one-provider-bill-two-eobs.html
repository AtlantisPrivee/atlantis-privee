How to Reconcile One Provider Bill With Two EOBs

✍️ Atlantis Editorial 📅 November 10, 2026 ⏱ 2 min read

A clinic sends one statement, but your health plan shows two explanations of benefits, or EOBs. Before you add the patient amounts, decide why there are two notices. One might replace an earlier decision. They might describe two services on the same account. They could also come from different insurers that processed the same service in sequence.

Match the scope of each notice

Lay out the bill and the two EOBs. Record the patient, provider, service date, service description, claim reference and processing date. Start with a particular charge on the bill, then find the line that represents it on each notice. Use the itemized version if the bill shows only the current account total. CMS explains the main fields on an EOB and separately identifies the account information and payments on a bill. CMS explanation of benefits guide · CMS medical bill guide

If the same plan processed both, ask which decision is active for the line and whether one voided or replaced the other. If two plans appear, identify which processed first and whether the second notice reflects that payment. If two distinct visits appear, keep a separate subtotal for each visit.

Assign a row to each service

For an illustration, the first EOB assigns $80 for a May 4 visit. The second assigns $50 for the same visit after a correction. The plan confirms the $50 decision replaced the $80 decision. Use $50 as the current responsibility for that line. A separate $20 charge for May 11 on its own EOB may belong in the account total, subject to credits already posted.

Put the active amount next to the provider's current charge and any amount you paid. Ask the billing office to confirm that its ledger uses the same decisions. The medical bill and EOB comparison tool can help lay out those amounts by line.

If you cannot match a charge to either EOB, keep it on a separate row and ask for the payer's claim reference or the date the provider sent the claim. That row remains open while you reconcile the charges with confirmed references.

If the provider sent a correction, check how the corrected claim changed your share. If two plans handled the same service, trace the primary payment through the secondary EOB.
