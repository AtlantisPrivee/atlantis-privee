Five Insurance Adjuster Requests Worth Clarifying

✍️ Atlantis Editorial 📅 June 11, 2026 ⏱ 3 min read

An adjuster can ask for records, explain policy terms, and communicate offers. The same request may mean something different depending on whose insurer is asking and which part of the claim is being evaluated. A useful response identifies the specific document, deadline, and coverage rather than guessing why someone asked. NAIC auto claim guidance

An early offer

Ask which losses the payment resolves and request the proposed release. A repair payment, a bodily injury offer, and a proposed full release should have separate entries in your file. If treatment or a repayment demand is unresolved, identify what remains open and who would bear it under the proposed terms. California insurance department auto claims guide · CMS beneficiary recovery process

A request to record your account

Ask what topics it covers, who will receive the recording, and how to request a copy. If the request comes from your insurer, check any applicable duty under the policy. Give accurate facts and label what you do not remember. The guide to talking with an adjuster offers ways to answer and confirm the next step.

Permission to obtain records

Ask for the purpose, record type, dates, and recipients. You may need to provide a particular report or authorization depending on the coverage, request, and procedural setting. Save the actual document you signed. HHS's access guidance explains how individuals can generally obtain their own health records from covered entities, subject to exceptions. HHS right to access health information

A deduction from a valuation

Ask what changed between the comparable vehicle and your vehicle, what evidence supports the adjustment, and where the dollar amount appears in the report. Keep the condition deduction separate from the deductible, taxes, and other lines. The Washington total-loss case shows how to read a condition adjustment. The Deduction Question Builder helps turn each unexplained line into a written question.

An update that says the claim is still under review

Ask what issue is pending, what item is missing, and when you should expect the next communication. Keep a dated record of requests and replies. California publishes specific claim-handling rules through its insurance department. The exact obligations depend on the state and claim. California fair claims settlement regulations

Paste a short, redacted message into the Insurance Letter Phrase Checker to generate neutral follow-up questions. If the insurer gives conflicting reasons or repeatedly leaves a material issue unresolved, see how to document a possible claim-handling problem.
