How to Check a Disputed Lien Still Held in Trust

✍️ Atlantis Editorial 📅 November 10, 2026 ⏱ 2 min read

You received part of your settlement while a lien or repayment claim is still disputed. Ask for the amount actually held for that issue and the accounting for everything already distributed. Then identify the event that will determine how the remaining money is paid.

Identify the dispute and the amount held for it

Ask for the claimant's name, account or reference, amount requested, amount disputed and amount reserved. Find out whether the dispute concerns the services listed, the amount claimed or the entity entitled to payment. Ask who is handling it and what documents are still needed.

ABA Model Rule 1.15 describes keeping property that is subject to competing interests separate until the dispute is resolved and distributing portions that are undisputed. California Rule 1.15 has its own provisions on accounting and prompt distribution of undisputed funds to the entitled person. Check the rule and facts that apply where your lawyer practices. ABA Model Rule 1.15 on disputed property · California Rule 1.15 on undisputed funds

Ask for the three amounts that reconcile the settlement

Consider a hypothetical $32,000 deposit. The firm pays $10,000 in agreed fees and costs, sends you $19,000 and holds $3,000 for the disputed claim. Those three amounts total $32,000. If the dispute resolves for $2,250 and the remaining $750 is due to you, the final accounting should show both distributions and the resulting zero balance for this example.

Ask whether any other sum is separately held. For each item, request the date it was reserved and the record needed to resolve it, such as a written payoff, withdrawal of a demand or an agreement on the disputed services. Ask when you will get the updated statement after funds are released.

Treat the current payment and future disbursement as separate entries

Use the Net Settlement Calculator to model the current payment and different possible outcomes for the holdback. Keep the assumptions visible until the dispute is resolved. For the amounts that have already moved, match the check to the client ledger. If the hold is based on a preliminary number, ask where the lien estimate came from and when it will be replaced.
