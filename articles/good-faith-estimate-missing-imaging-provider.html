The Good Faith Estimate Left Out a Separate Imaging Provider

✍️ Atlantis Editorial 📅 November 7, 2026 ⏱ 2 min read

You scheduled care as a self-pay patient. The clinic gave you an estimate, then a separate radiology group billed for reading the images. Ask who billed each component and which entity's charges your estimate listed. The estimate and bill need to correspond to the same provider or facility before you compare the amounts.

Find the billing entity for each charge

Keep the dated estimate and the initial bills from the clinic and the imaging group. Write down the provider or facility name, service date, description and account reference for each line. Ask the scheduling office whether imaging was part of the arranged care and whether the scan and interpretation were expected to be billed separately. Ask the imaging group whether it issued its own written estimate and for a copy if it did.

CMS's current consumer guide explains that an estimate may cover a single provider or facility even when several participate in scheduled care. Federal guidance describes enforcement discretion for some estimates that omit co-provider or co-facility charges while further rulemaking is pending. These sources apply to the uninsured or self-pay estimate process. CMS guide to good faith estimates · HHS co-provider guidance

Compare each bill with its own estimate

For an example, the facility estimates $900 and bills $930. A radiology group then bills $520 for the interpretation. The facility's difference is $30. The radiology charge needs to be compared with the group's written estimate if one exists. Combining both bills and subtracting the facility's $900 estimate would mix two providers.

CMS says the federal patient-provider dispute process may be available if a provider or facility bills at least $400 more than its good faith estimate and the other requirements are met. The initial bill must be within the applicable 120-calendar-day filing window. If you have no written estimate from the billing provider, CMS explains how to submit a complaint and ask about other options. CMS patient-provider dispute instructions

Ask for a written reconciliation

Ask both billing offices to confirm the roles, itemized amounts and any estimate they sent. Use the Claim Expense and Receipt Tracker to separate the clinic, imaging facility and interpreting group's entries. If repeated dates are part of the confusion, see how to compare lines on an overnight hospital bill. For care billed to a health plan rather than self-pay, check the insurer's processing and the provider's current balance.
