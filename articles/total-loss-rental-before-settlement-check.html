Your Total-Loss Offer Arrived Before the Check. When Does the Rental End?

✍️ Atlantis Editorial 📅 November 7, 2026 ⏱ 3 min read

The insurer emails a total-loss offer on Monday. The check has yet to arrive, and the rental company calls about a return date. Ask the carrier to identify the event that ends its payment for the rental. An offer, acceptance, dispatch of the settlement, receipt of the check and exhaustion of your rental benefit can occur on different days.

Find the dates and the balance of the benefit

Get the rental authorization, daily limit, total limit and the amount used so far. Ask whether the authorization comes from your own rental reimbursement coverage or from someone else's liability carrier. Ask which policy language or claim rule the carrier is using and the date and time the rental company has been told to stop direct billing.

If there is a settlement payment, request the date it was sent, the delivery method and the payee. A check naming a lender can reach a different recipient than you expected. Record the time the rental company expects the vehicle back and its daily charge after the authorization ends.

Apply the rule that matches your claim

Washington's rule effective October 18, 2026 addresses first-party claims where the amount of loss has been agreed and rental coverage is available. It provides for use of the rental coverage through the shorter period of seven calendar days after payment is sent to the claimant or until the coverage is exhausted. The Washington insurance regulator explains the update for consumers. WAC 284-30-391(7) · Washington insurance regulator's rule summary

For an illustration under those conditions, assume the carrier sends the payment on November 10 and the rental coverage has four eligible days left. Exhaustion may come before the seven-day point, regardless of when you open the mail. Ask the carrier to state the covered return deadline in writing. California's consumer guide separately emphasizes the terms of the purchased rental coverage. Use the policy and rules for your own claim. California auto claim guide

Put the possible out-of-pocket days on a calendar

If the insurer and rental company give different dates, ask them to confirm the same cutoff and who will pay for each day. Use the Rental Budget Stress Test to compare the cost of returning the vehicle on the confirmed date with any extra days you would pay for. Enter the rental firm's actual daily price and fees.
