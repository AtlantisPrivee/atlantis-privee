How to Follow Up on a Repair Supplement With No Response

✍️ Atlantis Editorial 📅 November 10, 2026 ⏱ 2 min read

Your repair shop found more damage, sent a revised estimate and is waiting for the insurer to respond. Ask the shop for a copy of the exact supplement and its delivery record. Then direct your follow-up at the current reviewer and ask which item is holding up a decision.

Find the revision the insurer should be reviewing

A supplement is a revised repair request that can include additional damage, parts or labor identified after the first estimate. Record the revision number and submission date. Ask the shop whether the amount shown is the new total or the increase over the approved estimate. Texas's insurance department explains that a shop can send a new estimate for additional repairs and that the insurer pays for the extra work if it agrees with the estimate. Texas Department of Insurance auto claims guide

Check the attachment list for teardown photos, parts prices and labor notes. Ask the shop whether it is waiting to order parts, continue work or schedule a reinspection. That helps you explain the practical consequence of the delay.

Give the reviewer one specific request

Suppose the first estimate is $2,460 and revision S2 adds $640 for a damaged bracket and related labor. You could write, “Shop B submitted supplement S2 on June 10 under claim 4218. Please confirm it is assigned to a reviewer. Can you tell us when to expect an itemized response, or give the shop a date for reinspection?” Include the delivery confirmation if you have it.

California's auto claims regulation has separate provisions for insurer inspections or reinspections in response to a supplement and for requests for photos or estimates. Check the provision that applies to your claim and use the actual date the insurer received the request. California supplemental estimate standards

Track the decision by line item

Ask for the approved amount and the items still open if the insurer accepts only part of the revision. Confirm the instructions for the shop in writing and save the new estimate alongside the earlier one. When a supplement is delayed by a missing image, ask which photos the adjuster reviewed. If several documents remain outstanding, send a short status list with an owner for each. Use the auto repair estimate comparison tool to align matching parts and labor across estimates.
