How to Reconcile Several Therapy Dates on One Invoice With the EOB

✍️ Atlantis Editorial 📅 November 10, 2026 ⏱ 2 min read

A physical therapy office bills a month's visits together. Your explanation of benefits, or EOB, has a separate line for each date and may show more than one service on some dates. Build the reconciliation visit by visit before you compare the invoice total.

Use the dates and codes together

Request the provider's itemized statement for the invoice period. List each visit, its service description or code where available, the units billed and any payment you made that day. Match the entry to the date, provider and line on the insurance notice. If one date appears on two claims, ask whether they cover different services or whether one replaces the other.

CMS's institutional billing training gives an example of how line-item service dates are reported for Medicare outpatient bills and inpatient Part B bills. For your therapy invoice, ask the provider how its billing system maps appointments and services to the claims the insurer received. CMS institutional billing guidance on line dates

Compare the visits before adding them

For an illustration, the current EOBs assign $25 for May 6, $25 for May 13 and $60 for May 20. The three active amounts total $110 before any credits. If the office statement lists $135, look for the extra $25 as its own line. It may come from a prior visit, a duplicate entry or another service. Verify the relevant dates, line references and amounts with the office.

If you paid $25 at the first visit, check where that receipt posted. CMS distinguishes the insurer's patient balance from the provider's record of payments and the amount currently due. CMS EOB guide · CMS medical bill guide

Ask the office to tie the invoice to the claim history

Use the medical bill and EOB comparison tool to compare the active responsibility and posted credits for each visit. If you have an invoice line without a matching EOB, ask for its claim reference and current status. Ask for a corrected itemization if a line is duplicated.

If your statement combines separate imaging charges, match the facility and interpretation claims by component. If two notices appear to apply to one invoice line, work out whether they replace each other.
