The Health Plan Denied Imaging After the Facility Changed

✍️ Atlantis Editorial 📅 November 7, 2026 ⏱ 3 min read

Your scan was approved at one location. The appointment moved, and the EOB now says authorization was absent or invalid. Compare the approval and the actual claim before concluding the plan rejected the scan itself. The facility, billing entity, procedure code or dates may differ between records.

Compare the order, approval and claim

Ask the ordering office for the written authorization, number, approved service, date range and approved location if listed. Ask the facility that performed the imaging for the claim details and denial notice, including the billing entity, service date, procedure code and the relevant identifier for the site or provider. Confirm what changed when you rescheduled and whether the change was submitted before service.

One example of a plan-specific workflow comes from EmblemHealth's radiology and cardiology FAQ for HIP members. It describes an authorization lookup that lists the site name and location as well as the authorization number and dates. Check your own plan's notice and the fields used in its authorization process. EviCore provider FAQ for EmblemHealth's HIP plans

Ask whether the problem is a claim field or a coverage decision

Suppose the same provider group operates two locations. The approval lists an outpatient imaging center. The scan was performed at the hospital, and the claim names the hospital facility. Ask the health plan which field triggered the denial and whether it will accept a correction, requires additional authorization documentation or treats the case as an appeal of a coverage decision. Ask the ordering office to confirm what updates it requested and keep any response.

If a coding or routing error occurred, ask the facility to explain its corrected-claim process and confirm when it submits the correction. If the carrier maintains the denial, follow the appeal instructions on the notice. CMS explains internal appeals and when independent review may be available for eligible disputes. Verify the applicable deadline while a facility correction is pending. CMS action plan for a denied health claim · HealthCare.gov guide to internal appeals

Send a short record of the change

Authorization 123 named Site A. The ordering office moved the appointment to Site B and the scan took place there on May 4. Please identify the field and rule behind the denial and confirm how the provider or member can have the claim reviewed.

Keep the request in the plan's secure channel. Use the Insurance Claim Date and Reminder Planner for dates shown on the notice and the Records Request Letter Builder to list the records you need. If an approval leads to a revised EOB, reconcile it with the current provider balance. If auto medical payments were sent first, trace them through the hospital's payment ledger.
