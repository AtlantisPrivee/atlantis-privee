Why a Filing Fee Was Deducted From Only One Settlement Payment

✍️ Atlantis Editorial 📅 November 10, 2026 ⏱ 2 min read

Your case settled through two payments. The first settlement statement deducts the full court filing fee, while the second has no filing fee line. Check the actual cost and how it was assigned before comparing the checks. A single cost can be collected from one payment while still belonging to the case as a whole.

Trace the charge from the court receipt to the statement

Find the receipt or cost ledger entry. Record the court, filing date, amount, case number and who paid it. Ask the firm to show whether it advanced the charge and whether the other disbursement contains any amount for the same receipt under a combined costs line.

The State Bar of California identifies filing fees as a type of litigation cost and describes questions about whether a client pays costs directly or reimburses costs the attorney advanced. California Business and Professions Code § 6147 also addresses what covered contingency agreements must say about how costs and disbursements affect the fee and recovery. California State Bar guide to costs · California contingency fee statute

Check the deduction and the fee base separately

Consider a hypothetical $12,000 first payment and $8,000 second payment. The firm advanced a $435 filing fee and recovers it from the first payment. The total case cost for that receipt is $435. With attorney fees left out of this example, the amounts after that cost are $11,565 and $8,000. Together they equal $19,565.

The calculation of the attorney fee is a separate check. If the agreement uses costs to adjust the fee base, ask which payment or total was used, and have the firm show the arithmetic. If the disbursements show a shared or prorated cost, compare the sum of the allocations with the invoice. Keep a clear reference from each portion back to the single underlying receipt.

Ask for a reconciliation you can keep

Request both itemized statements and the reference showing the amount collected against the receipt. Use the Settlement Deduction Questions to list the cost, supporting document and remaining question. If you paid the filing fee yourself, separate prepaid costs from sums reimbursed at closing. If only one coverage payment is subject to the contingency fee, check the scope of that fee separately.
