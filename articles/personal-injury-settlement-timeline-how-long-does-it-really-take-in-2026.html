Personal Injury Settlement Timeline: What Can Move a Case Forward?

✍️ Atlantis Editorial 📅 June 25, 2026 ⏱ 3 min read

Two injury claims can begin on the same date and move at very different speeds. One may involve an undisputed policy and complete records. Another may need additional treatment evidence, multiple insurers, or a court ruling before the parties can evaluate an offer. Track the events that actually happened and the specific records or decisions still pending. U.S. Courts overview of the civil case process

Keep three timelines in the same file

  • Care and work. Record appointments, changes in treatment, restrictions, and dates when the clinician expects to reassess. Keep the expected dates distinct from visits that already occurred.
  • Claim correspondence. Record notice, requests for records, submissions, written decisions, and offers. Save the exact documents and any proof of delivery. NAIC auto claim documentation guide
  • Legal and policy dates. Write down any filing, notice, appeal, or contract deadlines with their source. Negotiations and medical treatment can continue while a deadline approaches. Read the limitations guide for examples of different clocks. California Courts explanation of lawsuit deadlines

The Insurance Claim Timeline Builder sorts dated events while keeping the evidence attached. Use the Insurance Claim Date and Reminder Planner for dates you have independently confirmed.

Identify what is preventing the next decision

An insurer may be waiting for a specific item, such as a repair estimate, a medical record for one date, or clarification about which policy applied. Ask in writing what remains outstanding and what action comes next. Keep a dated copy of the request and response. If an adjuster says a claim is under review, ask which issue is being reviewed and whether any document is missing.

A potential settlement has its own steps. Review the offer, scope of release, parties who must sign, expected payment terms, and unresolved deductions. If a repayment demand is pending, record its stage. The Medicare recovery case explains why a conditional-payment list and a final demand can arrive at different points in the process. CMS beneficiary recovery process

Understand what filing adds to the timeline

Litigation may add service, scheduling orders, discovery, motions, mediation, and trial. Discovery can include document requests and testimony. In federal civil cases, the court describes several opportunities for parties to resolve disputes before trial. Procedures depend on the forum and case. U.S. Courts civil cases overview · California Courts civil discovery guide

For planning, write the next event as a task you can verify. For example, “Confirm receipt of the orthopedic report by Friday” is useful. “The insurer should settle next month” gives you no record to follow.
