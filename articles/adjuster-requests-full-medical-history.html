The Adjuster Asked for Your Full Medical History for One Injury Date

✍️ Atlantis Editorial 📅 November 10, 2026 ⏱ 2 min read

You reported an injury from one accident, and the adjuster asks for a release covering your full medical history. Ask which part of the claim the insurer is evaluating and which records it needs to do so. The practical issue is the scope of the request and how it relates to the actual injury and policy.

Get the request and form together

Ask the adjuster to identify the injury, provider, date range and type of record requested. For example, it may be seeking the emergency visit, follow-up treatment or earlier care for the same area of the body. Request the actual form and a written explanation of any policy requirement and deadline it is relying on.

When a covered health provider needs a HIPAA authorization to disclose information, the federal rule lists elements that include a meaningful description of the information, who may disclose and receive it, the purpose and an expiration date or event. Compare those fields with the insurer's stated request. HHS explains how an authorization permits specified disclosures. 45 CFR § 164.508 · HHS guide to authorization

Check whether a narrower packet answers the question

For an illustration, the carrier says it needs the emergency department note from June 9 and the orthopedist's follow-up note. The form names all providers over ten years. Ask whether those two records, a specified date range or a narrower authorization would meet the request. If the carrier says it needs additional records, ask which issue they would address.

Keep copies of any records sent and the signed version of any authorization. If the insurer treats signing as a condition of benefits, or if litigation or sensitive information is involved, get case-specific advice about scope and any response deadline.

Ask the adjuster which recipient will receive the records and how the files should be delivered. Use the verified secure upload route when the records include sensitive details, and keep a dated confirmation for the claim file.

Keep an index of the records and recipients

Use the Records Request Letter Builder to list the providers and dates under discussion. If the insurer also asks to speak to your employer, clarify which wage and schedule details it wants. If a billing record identifies the wrong member, verify the patient fields with the provider and plan.
