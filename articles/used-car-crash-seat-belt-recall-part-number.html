How to Check a Seat Belt Part Number Against a Recall After a Used Car Crash

✍️ Atlantis Editorial 📅 November 7, 2026 ⏱ 3 min read

A VIN search is the quickest way to look for an open recall. A used car's seat belt may have been replaced earlier, and a campaign may describe a pretensioner or buckle in a particular seating position. To identify the part that was present at the crash, connect the VIN to the installed assembly and its repair history.

Save the vehicle result with the date you ran it

Record the VIN from the vehicle and compare it with the VIN on the title or repair order. Save the dated search result, including the campaign number and any remedy information. NHTSA explains that the VIN search has limits, including recalls already repaired and some recently announced recalls for which affected VINs are still being identified. Its site also offers searches for recalls by vehicle and equipment. NHTSA recall lookup and search limitations

Ask the dealer or manufacturer for available campaign and service records for that VIN. If the records show an inspection or replacement, note the service date, seating position and part number on the repair order. A work order that just says “seat belt” leaves the particular component unresolved.

Read the part section of the actual campaign

Honda's November 2023 Part 573 report for campaign 23V-782 identifies affected vehicle populations and several front seat belt pretensioner part numbers. The remedy described in that report involved a dealer inspection and replacement where necessary. The published campaign shows why it matters to match the specific component and affected population rather than relying on a general description of a belt. Honda's Part 573 recall report filed with NHTSA

Ask a qualified technician to document any accessible label, installed position and condition of the assembly without disturbing it. Keep photos of the visible area and any previous replacement invoice. Avoid disconnecting or removing a restraint component yourself. If the vehicle is at a tow yard, request the custody and intake records first.

Record what the search actually established

A useful evidence note can read, “VIN lookup performed May 10. Manufacturer campaign shown as repaired in 2024. Repair order requested to confirm which assembly was inspected or installed.” That distinguishes a campaign status from a confirmed part identity. Keep any unclear serial, model or production-range information marked for verification. The recall report establishes the manufacturer's reported defect and remedy, and a vehicle inspection is needed to investigate what occurred in the particular crash.
