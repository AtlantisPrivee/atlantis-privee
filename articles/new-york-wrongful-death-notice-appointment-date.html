Which Appointment Date Starts a New York Wrongful Death Notice Period?

✍️ Atlantis Editorial 📅 November 7, 2026 ⏱ 3 min read

A family has a death certificate, a petition for administration and later letters appointing the estate's representative. For a New York wrongful death claim subject to the municipal notice requirement, the appointment record matters. Identify the date the court appointed the representative and the public entity to be served.

Use the court record for the appointment date

Collect the signed appointment order or decree, issued letters and court docket information. Keep the petition date and death date alongside them. If an order and the letters appear to show different dates, ask counsel or the court clerk to clarify the appointment record before calculating the deadline. Preserve any restriction or later amendment to the letters.

New York General Municipal Law § 50-e generally requires notice within 90 days of the claim arising where the statute applies. For wrongful death actions it measures the 90 days from appointment of the estate representative. The section also addresses who must be served and how. Check the rule for the particular public entity involved. New York General Municipal Law § 50-e

Track service and commencement independently

In Ramos v. New York City Transit Authority, an Appellate Division opinion identifies September 26, 2005 as the date letters of administration were granted. The court explained that a summons and complaint served within 90 days of appointment did not serve as the required wrongful death notice, and it permitted an amendment to a previously timely notice on the facts before it. That case illustrates why you should identify the document served, its recipient and its date. Appellate Division opinion in Ramos

Section 50-i separately sets a two-year period from death for wrongful death actions against the municipal entities it covers, subject to the applicable law. A claim for the decedent's own injuries can involve a different clock. Verify each claim, entity and deadline while you gather the records. New York General Municipal Law § 50-i

Keep proof of the specific document served

Record the notice version, service method, recipient, address and proof of service. Store it with the appointment documents. Use the Insurance Claim Date and Reminder Planner for dates checked against the applicable rule. If the entity is uncertain or the clock may be running, ask a New York lawyer promptly about the specific records and service options.
