How to Confirm a Spoken Policy Limit With the Adjuster in Writing

✍️ Atlantis Editorial 📅 November 10, 2026 ⏱ 2 min read

The adjuster says “the limit is $50,000.” Follow up with a written request for the coverage and exact limit that statement refers to. A per-person bodily injury limit, a per-accident limit, a property damage limit and a proposed settlement amount describe different things. The claim number alone may not identify which one you discussed.

Describe what you heard and request the underlying detail

Record the call date, adjuster and exact figure you understood. Ask which policy and coverage the figure belongs to, whether it is per person or per accident, and whether the carrier is describing the policy's stated limit or the amount currently available. Ask for the relevant declarations, coverage document or formal disclosure when available.

State insurance rules can give a claimant a formal way to ask. Florida Statutes § 627.4137, for example, describes a written request for a sworn statement from an insurer that does or may provide liability coverage and includes the limits of known policies in the required information. Use the rule that applies to your claim. Florida liability coverage disclosure statute

Keep a limit separate from an offer or a payment

For example, the adjuster mentions $50,000 on a call about bodily injury. Your follow-up can ask, “Was that the per-person bodily injury limit on the applicable policy, the amount you are offering on my claim, or a different figure? Please identify the coverage and confirm the limit in writing.” If other claims share the coverage, ask how the insurer distinguishes the policy limit from what it says is available.

The Texas Department of Insurance's consumer guide explains separate auto coverages and policy limits. Use the actual policy and confirmation to record what applies in your case. Texas Department of Insurance auto insurance guide

If the reply gives a single figure, ask the insurer to identify the unit. For bodily injury, a limit tied to one person can differ from the total available for the accident. Record each figure with the insurer's label and keep an offer or payment on its own line.

Keep the written record with the call note

Use the Insurance Claim Contact Log to record the original conversation and the insurer's reply as separate entries. If the insurer also mentioned a cutoff for responding, ask it to confirm the offer's expiration date. If a check arrived with an unclear description, verify the coverage associated with the payment.
