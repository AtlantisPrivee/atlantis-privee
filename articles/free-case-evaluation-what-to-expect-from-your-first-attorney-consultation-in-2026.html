What to Bring to a First Personal Injury Lawyer Consultation

✍️ Atlantis Editorial 📅 June 25, 2026 ⏱ 2 min read

Confirm how long the meeting will last, who will attend, and whether it is free or has a charge. A certified referral service can help locate a lawyer by practice area. The State Bar of California describes how certified referral services work and provides a directory for that state. Check the lawyer's license and public disciplinary information with the regulator in the relevant jurisdiction. State Bar of California referral service directory

Bring the documents that answer the first questions

Prepare a one-page chronology with dates and sources. Include the incident, first medical evaluation, insurance notices, and current status. Bring the report number, relevant policy or written denial, signed agreements, and any written deadline. List records you are still waiting for. The Insurance Claim Timeline Builder can sort the events without requiring you to share the entries online.

Ask how to provide records securely and whether the lawyer needs to check for conflicts before reviewing a full file. Redact unrelated identifiers from copies for an initial discussion when appropriate. Keep originals and label any redacted copy.

Ask what is being evaluated

  • Which claims, parties, or coverage questions appear relevant?
  • What facts or documents would change that assessment?
  • Which deadline needs immediate verification, and who will handle it?
  • Who would work on the case and communicate updates?
  • What work would start only after a representation agreement is signed?

A notice deadline for a public defendant can differ from an ordinary filing deadline. Take any government correspondence or incident report to the meeting and ask for the rule that applies. California Courts guide to government claims · Guide to checking filing deadlines

Review the fee agreement line by line

Ask how a contingency fee is calculated, whether litigation expenses are taken before or after the percentage, and who owes expenses if the case ends without a recovery. Ask whether the percentage changes with filing, trial, or appeal and what the agreement covers. The California State Bar distinguishes attorney fees from costs and explains common billing arrangements. Read the written agreement for your case. State Bar guidance on fees and billing

Bring a written offer if you have one. The Net Settlement Calculator can compare alternative fee bases and deductions after the lawyer explains the actual terms. For situations that may need prompt legal review, see when to speak with a personal injury lawyer.
