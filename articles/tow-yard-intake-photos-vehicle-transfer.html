Request Tow Yard Intake Photos After Your Car Moved Between Lots

✍️ Atlantis Editorial 📅 November 7, 2026 ⏱ 3 min read

The lot that holds your car today may only have photos taken after a transfer. Find the facility that first accepted it, then use the transfer record to match the same vehicle at the second lot. Those two sets of photos answer questions about condition at different moments.

Start with the first tow ticket

Pull the VIN, tow ticket number, pickup time and destination from the first record. Ask the tower whether it delivered the vehicle to that address or to a different facility. Ask each facility for its intake identifier, date and time, original photos if available, and the condition or inventory sheet associated with that arrival. Give a date range if their intake was overnight.

Texas's licensing department describes records a licensed vehicle storage facility must keep, including the date and destination of a vehicle transfer and identifying information for the tower. Its guidance also covers how an owner or authorized person can access a vehicle. Ask for copies of existing images and clarify the facility's release procedure. The Texas recordkeeping rule does not assure that intake photographs were taken. Texas TDLR storage records guidance · Texas TDLR vehicle access guidance

Identify the missing point in the chain

In one example, a vehicle arrives at Lot A at 9:15 p.m., leaves the next morning and arrives at Lot B before noon. A photograph from Lot B showing a damaged mirror documents its condition after arrival there. Look for Lot A's arrival photos, the pickup record for the transfer, and Lot B's first condition entry before trying to place the damage at a specific point. Record the source of each timestamp and flag gaps.

Ask the transfer operator for dispatch, pickup and delivery records and any photos taken while loading or unloading. If there are no pictures, invoices, a condition notation or a release receipt may still narrow the window. Ask whether relevant footage exists and whether it can be preserved, since its availability can change.

Request a record package by VIN and event

Please provide any intake photos and the condition or inventory sheet for VIN ending 4821 on May 4, plus the May 5 release and transfer record naming the receiving facility. Please include the time shown in the original record and identify the lot where it was made.

Use the Records Request Letter Builder for separate requests to each facility. Keep originals in separate folders so the date, lot and source stay clear. If rain changed the car's appearance, compare pre-tow and later storage photos. If the first tow ticket is also your strongest contemporaneous record of the crash, see the hit-and-run documentation workflow.
