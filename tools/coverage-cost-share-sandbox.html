How this tool works

Modeled share = minimum of remaining out-of-pocket space and [minimum(allowed amount, remaining deductible) + coinsurance × amount above that deductible].

What to check

Real plans can have separate individual or family limits, copays, exclusions, and services that do not count toward a limit. This simplified model is not a benefits determination.

  • CMS: Read an explanation of benefits

Worked example

For $1,000 allowed, $300 deductible remaining, and 20% coinsurance, the pre-limit share is $440.
