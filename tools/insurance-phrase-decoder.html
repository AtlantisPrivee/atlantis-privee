How this tool works

Eight explicit text patterns look for common phrases concerning offers, statements, releases, timing, valuation, coverage, responsibility, and record requests. Matches produce clarification questions.

What to check

This is not AI legal analysis. It can miss unfamiliar wording or flag an innocent phrase, and it does not classify a person’s motives.
