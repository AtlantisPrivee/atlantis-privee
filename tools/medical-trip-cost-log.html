How this tool works

Trip estimate = round-trip miles × your rate + parking + tolls. The report retains each date and purpose so you can compare the calculation with receipts.

What to check

Do not count both a full mileage estimate and all underlying fuel or vehicle costs without checking for overlap. The calculation does not establish tax deductibility or insurance coverage.

Worked example

A 24-mile trip at $0.30 per mile with $8 of parking and tolls produces a $15.20 planning estimate.

How your information is handled

Calculations run in this tab. The form does not upload your entries. Reports are saved to this tab’s workspace only when you choose to add them.
