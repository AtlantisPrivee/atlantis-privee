How this tool works

Minutes are summed and divided by 60. If you choose to enter an hourly planning value, total hours are multiplied by that value in a separate output.

What to check

Keep this log separate from actual missed-work records to avoid double counting. The tool does not establish entitlement to compensation for administrative time.

Worked example

Forty-five minutes sorting bills plus 25 minutes on a call produces a 70-minute log, or about 1.17 hours.

How your information is handled

Calculations run in this tab. The form does not upload your entries. Reports are saved to this tab’s workspace only when you choose to add them.
