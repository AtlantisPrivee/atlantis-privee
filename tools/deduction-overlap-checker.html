How this tool works

Underlying source IDs are normalized and grouped. Groups with more than one deduction line are flagged, and the full entered amount within each repeated group is displayed.

What to check

A repeated reference is a review prompt rather than proof of double counting. Confirm bill versions, sub-items, payment history, and any actual obligation.

Worked example
