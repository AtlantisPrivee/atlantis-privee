How this tool works

Visit rows are sorted by date and annotated with intervals. A follow-up item is counted when a summary is not marked available or a record reference is missing.

What to check

The view does not access health records and cannot verify that this is a complete care history. Administrative record gaps are different from gaps in treatment.
