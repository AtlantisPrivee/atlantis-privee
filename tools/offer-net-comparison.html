How this tool works

For each offer: net = gross × (1 − gross-based fee percentage) − case costs − repayments. The report compares these arithmetic nets on a common basis.

What to check

Enter consistent, verified deduction assumptions. The tool does not assess release language, taxes, future costs, case strength, or whether an offer is fair.
