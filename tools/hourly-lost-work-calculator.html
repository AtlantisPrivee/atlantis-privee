How this tool works

Regular amount = unpaid regular hours × rate. Overtime amount = unpaid overtime hours × rate × your entered multiplier. The two components are then added.

What to check

The tool does not decide whether overtime would have been worked or whether a loss is recoverable. Support the schedule and rates with records.

Worked example

At $24 per hour, 32 regular hours and four overtime hours at 1.5× produce $912 gross.
