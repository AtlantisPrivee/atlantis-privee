How this tool works

Each installment is discounted independently using days from valuation ÷ 365. The discounted payments are added and compared with a lump sum assumed available on the valuation date.

What to check

This simplified arithmetic is not an annuity quote or financial recommendation. Actual products and settlement structures can have material conditions beyond the payment amounts.

Worked example

Two $10,000 payments one and two years away have a $20,000 nominal total but a smaller present value under a positive rate.
