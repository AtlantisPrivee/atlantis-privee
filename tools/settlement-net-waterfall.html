What this tool helps you do

See how entered fees, expenses, and repayments change a gross offer into a net arithmetic scenario. Two identical gross offers can produce different nets when the fee basis or repayment assumptions differ.

How the result is built

The fee is calculated either on gross or on gross less entered case costs, according to your selection. All other entered deductions are then subtracted once.

What to check before relying on it

This is not an enforceability check, lien evaluation, tax calculation, or settlement valuation. Deductions can require professional verification and may change.

Where do my entries go?

Tool inputs are processed by code in this browser tab. Forms do not send your entries to a server. Optional page analytics are controlled separately in Privacy choices and do not include form values or report text. Workspace reports are saved only when you choose Add to workspace, in this tab’s session storage.
