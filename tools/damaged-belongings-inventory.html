How this tool works

Quantity multiplies both the entered purchase price and replacement estimate. The two totals remain separate and retain receipt-reference labels.

What to check

Age, condition, depreciation, deductibles, coverage limits, and evidence requirements are not modeled. A missing receipt label is a follow-up prompt, not a finding that an item is invalid.

Worked example

A $120 purchase and a $135 replacement estimate appear in different columns rather than being added together.
