How this tool works

At each monthly step the model adds balance × APR ÷ 12 plus the monthly fee, then applies the entered payment. It stops at payoff or 600 months.

What to check

This is arithmetic, not credit or financial advice. Confirm written terms and check whether billed charges or assistance options need review before agreeing to a plan.

Worked example

An $1,800 balance with no interest or fees and a $150 payment is paid off in 12 modeled months.
