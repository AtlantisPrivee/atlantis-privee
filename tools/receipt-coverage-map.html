How this tool works

The map subtracts entered reimbursements from each expense and totals amounts with no receipt label. It keeps gross expenses, reimbursements, and net figures separate.

What to check

An expense may be documented by records other than a receipt. Missing a reference does not mean the expense is invalid, and a net figure is not a claim valuation.

  • CMS: Check your medical bill for errors

Worked example

A $48 supply purchase with a $10 reimbursement contributes $38 net while remaining on the missing-reference list.

How your information is handled
