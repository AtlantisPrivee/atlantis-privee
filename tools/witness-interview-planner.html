How this tool works

Four general prompts are combined with one vantage-specific prompt and your own unresolved questions. The result is an interview outline, not a witness statement.

What to check

The tool cannot check consent, recording rules, memory accuracy, or litigation requirements. It does not recommend coaching a witness.
