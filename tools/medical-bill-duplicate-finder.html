How this tool works

Rows are grouped by service date, normalized provider and code, and charge rounded to cents. Only additional matching rows are included in the repeated-charge total.

What to check

This exact-match rule can miss duplicates described differently. A match is not a finding of an incorrect charge, fraud, or an amount you should withhold.

  • CMS: Check your medical bill for errors

Worked example

Two $180 EXAM rows with the same provider and date form one review group; a later follow-up remains separate.
