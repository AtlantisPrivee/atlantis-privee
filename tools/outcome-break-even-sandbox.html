How this tool works

Expected value = p × favorable net + (1 − p) × unfavorable net. The break-even probability solves certain net = p × favorable net + (1 − p) × unfavorable net.

What to check

This two-outcome abstraction omits timing, emotional costs, legal uncertainty, taxes, and individual risk preferences. Do not use it as advice to litigate or settle.

Worked example

With a $20,000 certain net, a $50,000 favorable net, and a −$5,000 unfavorable net, the mathematical break-even probability is about 45.45%.
