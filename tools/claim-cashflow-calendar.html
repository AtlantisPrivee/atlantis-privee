How this tool works

Starting from opening cash, the tool sorts movements by date and adds receipts or subtracts payments. The lowest balance is tracked after every entered movement.

What to check

The model cannot guarantee receipt dates, predict an insurer’s payment, or account for unentered transactions. For same-day payments, use the actual bank posting order when known.

Worked example

A payment before a reimbursement can create a temporary shortfall even if the ending balance later becomes positive.
