How this tool works

Offer rows are ordered by date. Each amount is compared with the prior recorded offer, while the first and latest entries define the total change.

What to check

Gross amounts may not be comparable when terms, deductions, or releases change. Retain the corresponding written proposal for each entry.

Worked example

Offers of $10,000, $12,500, and $14,000 show increments of $2,500 and $1,500, not a forecast of another increase.

How your information is handled

Calculations run in this tab. The form does not upload your entries. Reports are saved to this tab’s workspace only when you choose to add them.
