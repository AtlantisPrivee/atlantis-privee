How this tool works

Remainder = hypothetical starting amount × (1 − entered reduction percentage). Removed amount = starting amount × reduction percentage.

What to check

Do not treat this as a comparative-negligence eligibility calculator. Actual law can bar or change recovery and may not follow this simplified proportional model.

  • NAIC: State insurance departments

Worked example

A purely hypothetical 25% reduction to $20,000 removes $5,000 and leaves $15,000 before other deductions.
