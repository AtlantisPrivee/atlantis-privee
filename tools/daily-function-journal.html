How this tool works

Daily time difference = today’s minutes − usual minutes. Scores are averaged arithmetically; each entered day has equal weight.

What to check

Activities, pacing, and circumstances can change. The journal does not diagnose a condition, measure disability, or convert discomfort into a dollar value.

Worked example

Tracking the same meal-preparation task on two dates makes changes easier to discuss than comparing unrelated activities.
